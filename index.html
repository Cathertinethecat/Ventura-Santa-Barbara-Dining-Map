About the data

Restaurants (174 on the main layers, plus 69 on the Barbecue layer).

  • Award-recognized restaurants (Michelin Guide stars, Bib Gourmands and recommendations; Wine Spectator Restaurant Awards) across the coast from Santa Barbara to Malibu, plus the $$$$ and $$$ restaurants and the top-rated $$ sit-down restaurants (4.5 or higher with 300+ reviews) found in Santa Barbara, Montecito, Carpinteria, Ojai, Ventura, Santa Paula, Oxnard, Camarillo, the Conejo Valley, Calabasas, Malibu, Topanga, the San Fernando Valley, Burbank, Glendale, Eagle Rock, Atwater Village and the Santa Clarita Valley, from the Michelin Guide, Wine Spectator, OpenTable, TripAdvisor, Wanderlog, Restaurantji and local press, October 19, 2026 [1] [2] [3].
  • Price tiers come from the Michelin Guide, TripAdvisor or AAA where they publish one; where no source gave a price, the tier is inferred from the kind of restaurant and the card's note says so. OpenTable's price tags were not used because they show the top tier for nearly every restaurant here.
  • When a restaurant card opens, its rating, review count, price and current photo are fetched live from Google Places (Text Search and Place Photos) under the map owner's Google Maps Platform key; nothing from Google is stored [4].
  • Chains with more than about 20 locations are left out unless they are upscale steakhouse brands. Goleta, Fillmore, Piru, Port Hueneme, Simi Valley, Moorpark, La Cañada Flintridge and La Crescenta turned up no restaurant that met the tier rules with a confirmable address and position.
  • Positions are placed by address from published anchor points and are approximate; the research notes flag the weakest ones (Ventura side streets, Carpinteria Avenue, the Malibu PCH strip).
  • Link and closure check, October 19, 2026, done in the same research pass: every source link on a card was opened by the researcher, and places marked permanently or temporarily closed on their directory or Michelin page were left out.

Dining districts (38).

  • Walkable restaurant clusters: historic downtowns and main streets, lifestyle centers, harbor villages and the Malibu coast-highway strips; outlines drawn around the cluster, not legal boundaries, from published coordinates and street numbering [5].
  • Drive times are typical off-peak estimates from Plaza Park in downtown Oxnard, computed from road distance where no published figure was found.
  • District rosters (481 restaurants) come from each district's own directory plus Wanderlog, TripAdvisor, Yelp and Michelin lists; positions are schematic along the main street. Several districts came back thinner than the research target (Downtown Oxnard, Point Dume, The Oaks, Whizin Market Square, Porter Ranch, Magnolia Park) because few of their tenants have a published address on a page that could be opened.
  • Barbecue layer (69 places: 29 Korean BBQ and 40 American BBQ, including Santa Maria tri-tip and the regional Wood Ranch locations) from Wanderlog, Infatuation, Eater, local press, delivery listings and the restaurants' own pages, with a closure check in the same pass (seven closed places removed). A second verification pass on October 19, 2026 found no further closures; Yelp and Google Maps block the research tool, so Korean BBQ coverage in Sherman Oaks, Studio City and the west Valley may still be short.
  • Store rosters (115 stores at six centers) are partial: the centers' own directories could not be opened from the research tool, so the lists come from the developers' portfolio pages, Visit Oxnard, Santa Barbara visitor pages, local press and Wikipedia. The Luxury, Premium, Mid-market and Value tiers follow standard retail positioning and were assigned by the map team [6].
  • Rail: Metrolink Ventura County Line as drawn on the Ontario maps; Amtrak Pacific Surfliner runs on the same tracks and continues to Santa Barbara and Goleta [7].
  • City limits are the State Board of Equalization city boundaries served by the California Department of Conservation, with unincorporated county land shaded [8].
